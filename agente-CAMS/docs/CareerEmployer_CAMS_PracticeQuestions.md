## Dominio A — Riesgos y métodos del lavado de dinero y financiamiento del terrorismo

**Pregunta 2.**
In the context of money laundering, what is "smurfing"?

- A. The use of complex financial instruments to obscure the origin of funds
- B. The structuring of large transactions into smaller ones to evade reporting thresholds
- C. Transferring money through multiple countries to complicate tracing
- D. Investing illicit funds in real estate to legitimize their origin

✅ **Respuesta correcta: B**
**Explicación:** Smurfing is a method of money laundering where large transactions are broken down into multiple smaller transactions to avoid detection or suspicion by authorities, typically staying below a threshold that would trigger mandatory reporting.

- A. The company has operations in a high-risk jurisdiction.
